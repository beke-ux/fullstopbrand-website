about

Skateboarding is a sport, a lifestyle and an expression of creativity, freedom and courage. Yet the skate community is still often misunderstood and misrepresented. fullstopbrand is intentionally not tied to any specific scene, creating space for an open and inclusive community. Our brand is for everyone who has a passion for skateboarding – not about separating into cliques, but coming together as motivated, bold and creative skaters. To skaters who want to push each other, promote a skate culture of love and put a full stop to stereotypes.
